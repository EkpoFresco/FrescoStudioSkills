# Genre: Educational
Last checked: 2026-11-03. Sources at bottom.
Roblox fit: weak (commercially) - Education is tiny by live players (~4K CCU across ~1K games) [1], but Roblox funds and promotes it through curated sorts and grants [2][3].
Top Roblox references (mechanics only): few hits. Learning works on Roblox when wrapped in a real game loop; quiz-first experiences rarely chart (heuristic).
- Lua Learning (boatbomber): interactive coding tutorials with classroom assignments; early Roblox Community Fund grantee [4].
- Pathogen Patrol (Project Lead The Way, 2023): Community Fund-backed learning experience [5] (search excerpt only).
- Oregon Trail [new!]: edu-heritage concept as an exploration game, 3.8M visits (official status UNVERIFIED) [6].

## Core loop
- Play a mechanic that embodies the concept (simulate, build, test), get feedback, master, unlock.
- Classroom: teacher-led 20-45 min session, objective, activity, check for understanding, results (heuristic).
- Stealth learning: physics via building, economics via tycoons, history via exploration (heuristic).

## Session one (what works in a first session on Roblox, minute by minute where possible)
- Two audiences: students sent by teachers, and players arriving via discovery.
- Classroom: 0-5 min join a class server by code; 5-15 guided activity; 15-30 open challenge; 30-40 recap (heuristic).
- Discovery: play within 30 s like any other genre; never open with a quiz or text wall (heuristic).

## Retention hooks (D1 / D7 / D30)
- Classroom D1 / D7: the lesson sequence itself; the teacher is the retention system (heuristic).
- Public D1: badges and unlocks; D7: weekly challenges; D30: curriculum units and certificates (heuristic).
- Duolingo-style streaks work for self-learners; exam-like pressure does not (heuristic).

## Monetization norms (what players accept on Roblox for this genre)
- For experiences targeting schools, "monetization is prohibited during school hours" [3].
- Alternatives: Roblox Community Fund investments, grants and advances; off-platform teacher training and lesson resources [3].
- The fund launched with $10M in Nov 2021 [2]. Press later cites $25M (date UNVERIFIED) [7].
- Public edu-games: cosmetics only. Skip paid random items (heuristic).

## Social and discovery levers
- Learn and Explore curated sort; Roblox says games there "can expect more significant traffic and visibility". Developers can apply [3].
- Free one-on-one Education office hours with Roblox's team [3].
- Under-16 reach requires the all-ages path: age-verified creator, 2FA, maturity questionnaire, fee or Plus/Premium, and an evaluation needing 250 engaged unique plays in 60 days [8]. Keep content Minimal or Mild for Roblox Kids (5-8) [9].
- Chat is off by default under 9 and age-banded above [10]. Design for zero chat.
- Roblox Education's goal: 100M students by 2030 (UNVERIFIED current status) [5].

## Technical traps on Roblox
- School devices: Chromebooks, iPads, old laptops. Low part counts, light textures (heuristic).
- School networks may block Roblox. Ship offline lesson plans as backup (heuristic).
- One server per class via reserved servers [11].
- Track progress without collecting personal data; text passes Roblox filtering (heuristic).
- Localize; classrooms are multilingual (heuristic).

## What transfers from outside Roblox (what to keep, what breaks on Roblox's young, mobile, short-session audience)
External titles are common design references, described from general knowledge (heuristic).
- Keep: Minecraft Education sandbox-plus-lessons; Kahoot live quiz energy; Duolingo streaks; Prodigy quiz-as-combat; Oregon Trail consequences.
- Breaks: lecture text, worksheets rebuilt in 3D, quiz-first loops.
- Adapt: concepts as mechanics, short sessions, teacher dashboards off-platform.

## Octalysis read (dominant drives, 2-4 bullets)
- CD2 Accomplishment: badges and mastery.
- CD3 Creativity: build and test.
- CD1 Epic Meaning: real-world relevance.
- CD5 Social: class competition.
